Fuel sourcing inside the same workflow

VLSFO, LSMGO and MGO enquiries raised against the vessel profile, with port availability, indicative pricing windows, and the same agreement and audit layer as every other booking. The enquiry runs on the platform: raise one, compare what comes back, take a price.

Raise an enquiry against a vessel and the platform prices it with the suppliers that lift at that port — side by side, all in, with the validity window each price holds for. Take one and the stem is confirmed on the platform, delivery and bunker delivery note included.

A bunker price holds for hours, not days, and a supplier will not lift below its minimum stem. Both are on the comparison, because a price you cannot take is not a price.
