Why this works for GAC

The discount is a cost; the consolidated service revenue it attracts is the prize. The pass-through stays a fraction of the in-house work it consolidates — that asymmetry is the commercial case.

The worked example

£500,000 of GAC service spend on Full Stack saves £35,000 a year. GAC Assets and GAC Procurement are included at any tier; the discount never applies to third-party spend or the existing disbursement arrangement.
