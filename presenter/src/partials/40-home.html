The problem

Sourcing offshore services still runs on phone calls

Slow

A single crane hire means two hours of calls and voicemail before the first price arrives.

Opaque

Quotes arrive by phone and email in different shapes — never side by side, never comparable.

No data

Decisions leave no record. What was paid last time, and to whom, lives in someone’s inbox.

Manual compliance

Certificates are checked by hand, if at all — and expiry dates are discovered too late.

A compliance gate at the front and an expiry early-warning behind it. Lapsed suppliers cannot be booked — by anyone, anywhere.
