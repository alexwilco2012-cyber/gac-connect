What the proprietary SVS adds

Keyword search across 40+ service categories · supplier self-service portal · automated expiry alerts · live performance ratings fed from completed platform transactions, each shown with the number of ratings submitted · the GAC Verified badge as a visible mark of quality · GAC Gold Band as the paid annual audit tier above it. GAC Gold Band

A paid annual audit above standard verification, open to Premium suppliers at £1,500 a year: documentation, insurance, performance history and a site visit. Suppliers who pass carry the Gold Band badge in the marketplace, so clients see at a glance which suppliers GAC has looked at most closely. Standard verification stays free.

Renewed annually. No supplier can advertise their way into it, and it comes off if compliance lapses — the same rule that governs every other badge on the platform.
